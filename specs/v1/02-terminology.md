# Terminology

- **Agent**: software acting on behalf of a principal or organization.
- **Principal**: entity whose authority or funds are being represented.
- **Payment Intent**: structured expression of a requested financial operation.
- **Spending Policy**: financial rules determining whether an intent may consume funds.
- **Payment Instrument**: mechanism used to execute a payment, such as a wallet balance or virtual card.
- **Payment Decision**: result of evaluating an intent against authority, policy, risk, and approval requirements.
- **Transaction**: financial operation after execution has been initiated.
- **Settlement**: completion or reconciliation of funds movement through an external rail.
- **Refund**: reversal or return of funds associated with a transaction.
- **Ledger**: authoritative record of financial accounting events maintained by an Agent-Pay implementation.
- **Approval**: explicit human or delegated control required before execution.
- **Payment Rail**: external mechanism through which funds are transferred or payment is accepted.
