# AI Context — Agent-Pay

Agent-Pay is the **financial control and payment execution layer for the agentic internet**.

## Relationship to ATF

Agentic Trust Foundation establishes identity, delegation, authorization, trust, consent, revocation, provenance, and authority evidence.

Agent-Pay consumes that authority and applies financial controls:

- policy
- budgets and reservations
- approvals
- payment authentication
- payment instruments
- provider execution
- transactions and double-entry ledger
- settlement
- reconciliation
- audit and notifications

**Agent-Pay must never expand upstream ATF authority.**

## What Agent-Pay is not

Agent-Pay is not:

- a bank;
- a payment processor or payment rail;
- a general identity/trust protocol;
- a general-purpose agent framework;
- a replacement for ATF;
- a replacement for OAuth, MCP, A2A, or other authoritative protocols;
- proof of a live external PSP/bank/card-issuer integration.

## Evidence rule

Protocol contracts, reference implementations, provider simulators, automated tests, and CI passes are engineering evidence. They are not automatically independent production, adoption, or certification evidence.

See the ATF repository's canonical PROJECT.md, WHY.md, AI-CONTEXT.md, and STATUS.md for the ecosystem-level interpretation.
