# Non-Goals

Agent-Pay is not intended to become:

- A general identity provider.
- A global trust authority.
- A general-purpose authorization framework.
- An enterprise IAM replacement.
- A bank account provider by definition.
- A mandatory wallet implementation.
- A mandatory virtual-card provider.
- A payment processor tied to one provider.
- A merchant marketplace.
- A cryptocurrency or blockchain project by requirement.
- A replacement for the Agentic Trust Foundation.

Agent-Pay may integrate with these systems when useful, but integration does not transfer ownership of their concerns into Agent-Pay.
