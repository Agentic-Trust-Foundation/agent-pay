# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Agent-Pay is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and inclusive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique implementations and evidence rather than people.
- Keep discussions technically relevant.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and community. Security concerns should be reported privately as described in `SECURITY.md`.
